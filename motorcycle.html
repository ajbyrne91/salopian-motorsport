Carburettor Cleaning & Rebuild

Over time, carburettors accumulate varnish, dirt, and debris that restrict fuel flow and cause rough idling or poor performance. We fully disassemble, ultrasonically clean, and rebuild your carbs using high-quality parts to ensure smooth throttle response, proper fuel delivery, and optimal engine performance.

Valve Clearance Adjustment

Incorrect valve clearances can lead to hard starts, poor fuel economy, and long-term engine damage. We precisely measure and adjust clearances to factory specs, ensuring your engine breathes correctly and maintains peak power and reliability.

Chain & Sprocket Replacement

A worn chain and sprockets can affect acceleration, handling, and rider safety. We inspect, remove, and replace your chain and sprockets using top-tier components, then tension and lubricate everything to manufacturer standards.

Brake System Overhaul

From spongy levers to noisy discs, brake issues are no joke. We service or replace pads, calipers, discs, and fluid, ensuring confident stopping power and consistent performance on road or track.

Fork Seal Replacement & Suspension Service

Leaky forks compromise handling and rider comfort. We strip and rebuild front forks with fresh seals and oil, dialling in the damping and rebound to suit your riding style—whether it’s weekend touring or track-day aggression.

Electrical Fault Diagnosis & Repair

Whether it’s dead batteries, blown fuses, or mysterious no-starts, we trace wiring faults using specialist tools and expertise. From charging system tests to full re-wiring jobs, we get your bike lit up and road-ready.

Engine Rebuilds & Repairs

From top-end refreshes to full strip-down rebuilds, we restore your engine with precision machining, quality parts, and race-grade standards. Whether it’s knocking, smoking, or just tired, we’ll bring it back to life.

Clutch Replacement & Adjustment

If your clutch is slipping, dragging, or feeling inconsistent, it may be time for an overhaul. We inspect plates, springs, and cables/hydraulics, replacing worn components and restoring smooth gear engagement and consistent bite.

Tyre Fitting & Wheel Balancing

We supply and fit a full range of motorcycle tyres—from sticky sport compounds to all-weather touring rubber. Every tyre is fitted with care and dynamically balanced to ensure stability, grip, and long life.

Motorcycle Tyres

Michelin is known for cutting-edge innovation, exceptional grip, and long-lasting compounds. Their tyres offer excellent wet-weather performance and consistent wear, making them a top choice for both road riders and track-day enthusiasts.

Bridgestone offers a versatile range of tyres known for comfort, precision, and durability. From touring to aggressive riding, their advanced compounds and tread designs provide excellent traction and stability across various conditions.

With deep roots in motorsport, Pirelli delivers high-performance motorcycle tyres with superb handling and corner stability. Perfect for sport and supersport riders, Pirelli tyres offer confidence-inspiring grip at high speeds.

Dunlop blends race heritage with everyday reliability. Known for sharp steering and responsive handling, their tyres perform well on the street and the track, with options for cruisers, adventure bikes, and supersports.

Designed specifically for motorcycles, Metzeler tyres offer fantastic grip and high mileage. Their focus on rider comfort and road feedback makes them a favourite among touring and performance bike owners.

British-made Avon Tyres are a trusted name for classic, cruiser, and custom motorcycle owners. Combining style with substance, Avon offers dependable grip, long tread life, and strong performance in all weather conditions.

Continental tyres are engineered for precision and control, offering strong all-weather performance and consistent feedback. Ideal for riders who want safety, comfort, and agility—whether commuting or touring.

Kenda provides affordable, durable tyres for everyday riding. Ideal for budget-conscious riders, their tyres perform reliably across a range of bikes including commuters, dirt bikes, and scooters.

Maxxis motorcycle tyres are known for off-road strength and all-terrain reliability. Popular among adventure and dirt riders, Maxxis offers great value without sacrificing grip or performance.
