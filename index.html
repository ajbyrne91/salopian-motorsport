About Us

Trusted Experts in Motorcycle & Car Repairs – Shrewsbury, Shropshire

At Salopian Motorsport, we live and breathe engines. Located in the heart of Shrewsbury, we are proud to be one of Shropshire’s go-to workshops for motorcycle repairs, car servicing, and premium tyre fitting. Whether you’re looking to fine-tune your superbike, replace worn tyres, or carry out full mechanical repairs, our team delivers precision work and real results — every time.

Specialists in Motorcycle Repairs

We’re not just mechanics. We’re riders ourselves — and we know how much trust it takes to hand over your bike to someone else. That’s why every motorcycle that comes through our doors is treated like our own.

From routine motorcycle servicing to complex electrical fault diagnostics and full engine rebuilds, we combine years of hands-on experience with cutting-edge tools and top-tier parts. Whether you’re dealing with rough idling, clutch issues, or just need a fresh set of tyres, our motorcycle repair service is second to none in Shrewsbury.


High-Quality Tyre Fitting in Shrewsbury

When it comes to safety, performance, and ride comfort, tyres matter more than you think. At Salopian Motorsport, we supply and fit a wide range of motorcycle and car tyres to suit all riding styles and road conditions. Whether you’re a sports rider, tourer, commuter, or drive a performance car, we’ll match you with the perfect set of rubber.

All tyres are professionally fitted, balanced, and tested right here in our Shrewsbury workshop. Looking for advice? We’ll talk you through the best options based on your bike, budget, and riding habits.


Repairs You Can Rely On

We believe in doing things properly — no bodge jobs, no guesswork. Whether it’s a leaking fork seal on your motorcycle, a misfiring engine in your car, or a full performance upgrade, our repair services are thorough, efficient, and built to last.

All work is carried out by trained professionals with a deep understanding of both modern and classic engines.


Local, Independent, and Rider-Focused

As a locally owned business in Shrewsbury, we’re proud to serve our community of bikers, drivers, and motorsport enthusiasts. We know the roads, we know the machines, and most importantly — we know what riders and drivers actually need.

With decades of combined experience, we’re committed to offering a more personal and professional service than the big chains. No upselling. No generic advice. Just honest workmanship and real results.
